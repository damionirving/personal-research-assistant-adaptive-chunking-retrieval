# AcmeCorp Employee Benefits Policy

Document overview:

- Company: AcmeCorp
- Version: 2026.4
- Published: March 15, 2026
- Classification: Internal Use Only

## Section 1: Office Equipment And Remote Work

### 1.1 Home Office Stipend

AcmeCorp provides a one-time remote work stipend of $1,250 to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive $600. You must use this money within the first 90 days of employment. It covers desks, ergonomic chairs, and monitors.

### 1.2 Tech Renewal Cycle

The company replaces standard employee laptops every 36 months. Employees can purchase their old laptop for a flat fee of $150. You must submit the buyout request exactly 30 days before your renewal date.

## Section 2: Health And Wellness Benefits

### 2.1 Gym Reimbursements

Employees get up to $75 per month for fitness expenses. This applies to gym memberships, yoga studios, and martial arts classes. It does not cover weight loss supplements or athletic apparel.

### 2.2 Mental Health Support

AcmeCorp partners with MindEase to offer 12 free therapy sessions per calendar year. This benefit extends to legal spouses and dependents under the age of 26.

## Section 3: Leave And Time Off

### 3.1 Sabbatical Leave

Employees earn a 4-week paid sabbatical after 5 consecutive years of service. You cannot split this time into multiple smaller trips. It must be taken all at once.

### 3.2 Floating Holidays

Every employee receives 3 floating holidays on January 1st. These days do not roll over to the next calendar year. Unused floating holidays expire on December 31st.
